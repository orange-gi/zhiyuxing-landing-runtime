Thank you for using 知与行

知与行 (“we”, “the service”) is operated by an individual developer. Please read this policy before using the service so you understand how we collect, use, and protect personal information.

1. How we collect and use personal information

1.1 Account registration and sign-in

You need to register and sign in before using related features.

  • Phone number or email: used for registration and sign-in
  • Password: the password you set
  • Third-party account information: if you sign in with Apple, we may receive public profile information (photo, nickname) after you agree

A phone number or email is required to meet real-name network rules in China. If you do not provide it, we may be unable to offer the service.

1.2 AI conversation and content analysis

When you use AI conversation:

  • Input: text, images, or voice you submit
  • Conversation history: your exchanges with the AI
  • Feedback: ratings you give to a reply

We analyze this information to understand your question and generate a reply.

We do not extract biometric identifiers such as voiceprints or facial recognition from voice or photos.

1.3 Records and plans

When you keep records or make plans:

  • Content you create: notes and plan descriptions
  • Labels: categories you choose

1.4 Personalized reports

When you generate a report:

  • Answers: questionnaire choices
  • Analysis results: personalized reports generated with AI

1.5 Operations and security

To keep the service stable we automatically collect:

  • Device information: model, OS version, device identifiers
  • Logs: IP address, access time, operation logs
  • Usage data: feature frequency and page visits
