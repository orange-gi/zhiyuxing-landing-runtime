Managing permissions

  1. Open Settings on iPhone
  2. Scroll to 知与行
  3. Open the app settings
  4. Turn each permission on or off

If you refuse or withdraw

  • Refuse: the related feature may be unavailable; other features still work
  • Withdraw: turn it off in system settings; the related feature will stop working
